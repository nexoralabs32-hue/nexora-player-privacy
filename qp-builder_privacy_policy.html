Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 20, 2026

Welcome to Question Paper Builder ("we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to respecting and protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy outlines how our mobile application collects, uses, and safeguards information when you use our app.

1. Information Collection & Usage

Question Paper Builder is designed to protect user privacy. We do not require user account creation, registration, or login. We do not collect, store, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII) such as your name, email address, or phone number.

2. Data Storage & Local Files

  • Local Document Storage: All question papers, templates, draft content, and user preferences are saved locally on your device.
  • Exported Documents: PDF and Microsoft Word (DOCX) files generated by the app are saved directly to your device's public Downloads folder under Downloads/Question Paper Builder.
  • Media Access: When you choose to attach images to question blocks, the app accesses the selected media files locally from your device storage solely to render them in your question papers. Your media files are never uploaded to external servers.

3. Third-Party Services & Technologies

Our app utilizes trusted third-party services that may collect pseudonymous device information to deliver core application functionality:

  • Google Mobile Ads (AdMob): We use Google AdMob to display interstitial and rewarded advertisements. AdMob may automatically collect pseudonymous data such as your device's Advertising ID, IP address, and general device telemetry to deliver non-personalized or personalized ads. For more information, please visit Google Privacy & Terms.
  • Google Play Services Downloadable Fonts: The app dynamically downloads official Google Fonts over the internet via Google Play Services (com.google.android.gms.fonts) to render document typography. Font requests are handled by Google Play Services in accordance with Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Device Permissions

To provide full functionality, the application requests the following Android permissions:

  • INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE: Required to load Google Fonts dynamically over the internet and serve AdMob advertisements.
  • READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE: Used exclusively to select and import images from your device gallery into question paper blocks.

5. Children's Privacy

Our application does not knowingly collect or solicit personal information from children under the age of 13. If you believe that a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can immediately take appropriate action.

6. Security

Because all your documents, templates, and content are stored locally on your device, your data security depends on your device's security settings. We recommend keeping your device operating system updated and protected.

7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
