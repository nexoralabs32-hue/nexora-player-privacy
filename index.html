Privacy Policy for Nexora Player

Effective Date: September 8, 2026

Nexora Labs ("we", "our", or "us") operates Nexora Player (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how Nexora Player accesses, uses, stores, and handles information when you use the App.

1. Information We Access

Nexora Player is a video player designed to play video files stored on your Android device.

To provide its core functionality, the App may access video and media files on your device when you grant the required Android permissions or select media through the Android system media picker.

The videos and media files accessed by Nexora Player are used solely to provide video playback and related player functionality.

Nexora Player does not upload your personal videos or media files to Nexora Labs servers.

2. Information We Collect

Nexora Player does not require you to create an account or provide personal information such as your name, phone number, or email address to use the App.

We do not operate a server that stores or uploads your personal video files.

3. Advertising

Nexora Player uses Google AdMob, a third-party advertising service provided by Google, to display advertisements.

Google AdMob may collect or use certain information, such as advertising identifiers, device information, IP address, approximate location information, and information relating to advertising interactions, in accordance with Google's policies and applicable laws.

Advertising information may be used to provide, measure, personalize, and improve advertisements, depending on applicable settings, consent, and regional requirements.

For more information about how Google handles information in its advertising products, please visit Google's privacy resources.

4. Third-Party Services

Nexora Player uses the following third-party service:

Google AdMob — used to display advertisements in the App.

Third-party services may independently process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

5. Video and Media Files

Nexora Player accesses video and media files on your device to allow you to browse and play your media.

Your video files remain on your device and are not uploaded to Nexora Labs servers by the App.

Nexora Player does not sell, rent, or share your personal video files with third parties.

6. Data Storage and Retention

Nexora Player does not maintain cloud storage for your personal video files.

Media files remain under your control on your device. The App does not upload your videos to Nexora Labs servers.

Information processed by third-party advertising services is subject to those services' respective policies and retention practices.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. Since your personal video files remain on your device and are not uploaded to our servers, Nexora Labs does not maintain copies of your personal videos on its servers.

Third-party services used by the App may apply their own security measures to information they process.

8. Children's Privacy

Nexora Player is not specifically directed toward children. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children through the App.

9. Your Choices

You can control the permissions granted to Nexora Player through your Android device settings.

You may also choose which media files to provide to the App where Android's media picker is available.

You may also control certain advertising preferences through your Android device and Google's available advertising settings.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.
